<div class="container page-section">
    <h1>Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
    <p class="lead page-intro">Quick answers about eligibility, participation, and the contest process.</p>

    <h2 class="faq-section-title">General</h2>

    <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>Is there a registration fee to participate?</h3>
        <p>No. Submission to the 2026 IEEE SciVis Contest is <strong>free</strong>. You do not need to pay or register separately to enter—use the provided data and submit your visualization.</p>
        <p>Registration for the <strong>IEEE VIS Conference</strong> itself is separate and handled by the conference organizers.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>Can I participate as a team?</h3>
        <p>Yes. You may enter as an <strong>individual</strong> or as a <strong>group</strong>. We suggest keeping teams to about six members or fewer so authorship stays clear and fair.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>Who can join the contest?</h3>
        <p>The contest is <strong>open worldwide</strong>. Students, researchers, industry professionals, and visualization enthusiasts are all welcome.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>When and where does the contest take place?</h3>
        <p>The submission deadline was <strong>July 31, 2026</strong>. Presentations for shortlisted entries are scheduled for <strong>Monday, November 9, 8:00–11:30am</strong> at IEEE VIS 2026 in Boston (November 9–13), with a coffee break from 9:30–10:00am. A virtual presentation option will be available.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>How do I know if I am shortlisted?</h3>
        <p>Shortlisted candidates will receive an invitation from the Program Chairs by the <strong>end of September</strong> to present at the SciVis Contest session, held alongside IEEE VIS. Invitations will include presentation details and logistics (in-person or virtual).</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>How do I join the challenge?</h3>
        <p>There is no separate registration form. The typical path is:</p>
        <ol>
            <li>Explore the <a href="{{ "/" | relative_url }}">contest website</a>, data, and example notebooks</li>
            <li>Work on one or more of the listed tasks (some are optional)</li>
            <li>Prepare your paper and video, then submit via Precision Conference</li>
        </ol>
        <p>See the <a href="{{ "/submission/" | relative_url }}">Submissions</a> page for requirements and status.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
        <h3>Do I need to complete every task?</h3>
        <p>No. You are not required to address all tasks:</p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Task 0</strong> is foundational exploration to get familiar with the data.</li>
            <li>For a competitive entry, we encourage tackling at least one of <strong>Tasks 1–3</strong>, ideally with interactive tools for exploration.</li>
            <li>You may also pursue your own creative direction, as long as you use the provided datasets and demonstrate originality, insight, and strong technical execution at scale.</li>
        </ul>
    </div>
