    <h2>What to Include</h2>
    <p>Each submission should include the following:</p>
    <ul class="requirement-list">
        <li>
            <strong>Paper (PDF)</strong>
            A 3–4 page manuscript describing your visualization and analysis techniques, plus one optional page for references. Focus on methods and results—avoid lengthy background or data descriptions. Follow the <a href="https://tc.computer.org/vgtc/publications/conference/" target="_blank">VGTC formatting guidelines</a> (LaTeX and Word templates available). Append explanatory images to the PDF. Maximum file size: 50&nbsp;MB.
        </li>
        <li>
            <strong>Video</strong>
            An MPEG, AVI, or QuickTime video (maximum 10 minutes) demonstrating your system, methods, or process in action.
        </li>
        <li>
            <strong>Authorship</strong>
            Individual and group submissions are both welcome. Include the full author list at submission time.
        </li>
    </ul>
    <p>Top entries will be invited to present at the contest. The best submission may also be invited to submit an extended version to <em>IEEE Computer Graphics and Applications (CG&amp;A)</em> for publication consideration.</p>
